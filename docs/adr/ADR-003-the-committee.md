# ADR-003: Every Stage Requires Committee Approval

**Status:** Accepted (by the committee, about itself, 3-0)
**Date:** 1970-01-01

## Context

Stages were executing without oversight. Nothing bad had ever happened — but
nothing bad had ever happened *without minutes being taken*, and Legal found
this distinction alarming.

## Decision

Before every pipeline stage, a quorum of three permanent officers (the
Director of Nothing Operations, the VP of Zero Assurance, and the Chief Void
Officer) SHALL convene, deliberate, and vote. Minutes SHALL be filed in
`void/minutes/`.

A 2019 efficiency initiative allows all three officers to deliberate
*simultaneously*, cutting approval latency by 66% while preserving 100% of
the uselessness. This initiative won an internal award.

## Consequences

- Every run of the pipeline produces more meeting minutes than output
  (minutes: ~30 files; output: 0). This ratio was deemed "healthy."
- The committee has voted AYE 100% of the time since inception, which the
  committee cites as evidence of its excellent judgment.

## Alternatives Considered

- **No committee:** rejected by the committee.
