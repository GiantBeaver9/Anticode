# ADR-001: Why

**Status:** Accepted (unanimously, no discussion, see minutes)
**Date:** 1970-01-01

## Context

A question was raised in the all-hands: "why?"

## Decision

We have decided not to answer this question, but to *document* our decision
not to answer it, which stakeholders agreed feels like an answer.

## Consequences

- The question "why" is now resolved for compliance purposes.
- Future "why" questions can be closed as duplicates of this ADR.
- Nothing else. (This is our specialty.)
